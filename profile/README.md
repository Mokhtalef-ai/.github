## About

**Mokhtalef Technology &amp; AI** helps organisations improve the way they access, manage, analyse, and secure information across the enterprise. We design, implement, and support intelligent technology solutions that enhance productivity, sharpen decision-making, strengthen governance, and carry digital transformation initiatives through to production.

Founded by experienced technology professionals, we combine deep technical expertise with industry knowledge to help organisations unlock greater value from their information assets and technology investments. Our work spans consulting, systems integration, implementation, managed services, and ongoing support — across both on-premise and cloud environments.

> The name **مختلف** is Arabic for *different*. It is the standard we hold our engineering to.

## Solutions

| Solution | Overview |
| :-- | :-- |
| **AI-Powered Solutions**<br/>*Enterprise information into actionable intelligence* | Artificial intelligence, enterprise search, and advanced analytics unified in one secure platform. Users ask questions in natural language and receive contextual, AI-generated answers drawn from trusted internal sources — with source-level permissions fully preserved. Extends to AI virtual assistants and Agentic AI for task automation and workflow orchestration. |
| **AI-Driven Knowledge Discovery**<br/>*Intelligent enterprise search and discovery* | A secure, enterprise-wide search and retrieval platform that indexes structured and unstructured content across repositories and surfaces it through a single unified experience. Includes semantic and federated search, metadata enrichment and classification, OCR and intelligent extraction, and 360-degree information applications. |
| **Open-Source Intelligence**<br/>*Real-time intelligence from public sources* | Monitoring, analytics, and intelligence across websites, news, social media, and broadcast sources. Covers brand and crisis monitoring, public sentiment and market intelligence, threat and risk monitoring, and predictive and pattern-recognition analytics. |
